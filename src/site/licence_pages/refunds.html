Who handles refunds

The {{product_title}} is sold through Paddle, who act as Merchant of Record and authorised reseller. Paddle processes the payment, the taxes, the invoice and the refund.

{{seller}} supplies the software and issues the license. It does not process card payments and does not refund them itself: when Paddle refunds a purchase, {{seller}} treats the license as ended.

How to request a refund

Contact Paddle with your order:

  • use the “View receipt” or order-management link in your Paddle confirmation e-mail; or
  • open a request at paddle.net and choose the refund option; or
  • follow the support path printed on your Paddle receipt.

You can also write to {{seller_email}} if you need help finding your order. {{seller}} can confirm the product or license details to Paddle; Paddle remains the party that pays the refund.

Statutory withdrawal

If you buy as a consumer, mandatory law can give you a right to withdraw within a set period, often 14 days in the EU, the EEA, Switzerland and the UK for eligible digital purchases. Paddle applies those rules as seller of record. The exceptions, including digital content supplied immediately with your express consent, are described in Paddle's refund policy.

Nothing here shortens or replaces your mandatory consumer rights.

Outside the statutory period

Paddle can grant a refund at its discretion under its own policy. Abuse of the refund process can lead to a refusal.

If {{name}} has a defect that prevents it from working as described, ask for help first so that it can be fixed. If it cannot be, contact Paddle with the details.

Effect on the license

When Paddle finally refunds the purchase, the license granted for it ends, subject to mandatory law. The key is not blocked technically: {{name}} checks keys offline and never contacts a server about them. A provisional chargeback alone does not end the license until the payment outcome is final.
