This site

This site is plain HTML and CSS. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from third parties, with one exception: on the pricing page, the Paddle checkout script is loaded only when you press the buy button.

When you buy a license

Paddle acts as Merchant of Record and processes the payment. It collects what a payment requires (name, e-mail address, billing details, tax information) under Paddle's privacy policy.

Once the payment is confirmed, a small service run by {{seller}} (a Cloudflare Worker) stores the name, e-mail address, order number, transaction id and the license. That is what the thank-you page shows and what the e-mail with your key is sent from. That e-mail goes out through Cloudflare's own mail service (Cloudflare Email Service), which handles your address and the message to deliver it, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. The service removes your name and your e-mail address {{retention_days}} days after the license was sent; the order number stays. Its logs hold event names, transaction ids, order numbers, statuses and error codes, never a name, an address or a key.

Nothing else is stored, and nothing is sold or shared beyond what issuing the license requires.

The controller of the record kept by that service is {{publisher}} (see the imprint for the address).

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what is stored about your purchase, or for its deletion, by writing to {{seller_email}}. A license whose record is deleted can no longer be sent again; the key you already have keeps working.

You can also contact Paddle about the data it holds for the payment, and your local data-protection authority if you think your rights are not respected.
