**A view's window follows the program's title bar setting**: with the program's own decorations (`window.system_title` off), a view in a window of its own has no system decoration; its header is the window's title bar (dragged, maximised by a double click) with the window's minimise, maximise and close at its right end, the same as the main window's; that close puts the view back where it belongs. With the system's title bar, the header sits under it as before. A change of the setting applies to the open view windows at once.
