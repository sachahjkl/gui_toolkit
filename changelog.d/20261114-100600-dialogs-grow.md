**A dialog grows to its title and its buttons**: a dialog that is not resizable is as wide as its title and its row of buttons on one line need, `width_em` at least, `DIALOG_MAX_WIDTH_EM` (46) and the window at most. Past that, its title wraps (no "…") and its buttons go onto more lines, at its right end. `ui.confirm` and `ui.prompt` are built of `dialog_body` and `dialog_buttons`.
  - `dialog_buttons` is a wrapping row with its own gap: a program removes the spacers it put between buttons.
  - `ui.flow` takes an `align` for its lines.
