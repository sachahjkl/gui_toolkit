**Update channels in the Updater and the standard Updates page.** A program whose releases come on channels (stable, beta) gives each channel's folder to `update.updater_start` (`Updater_Options.channels`), and `update.set_channel` follows another one at once: what the old channel offered is dropped, a check under way is made again. The Updates page shows the Channel setting only for such a program (`Standard_Defaults.update_channels`), as a choice of its channels; a program without channels no longer shows a free text field for it. `prefs_page.update_source` reads the address and the channel for the Updater (docs/update.md, "Channels").
