**`ui.strip`**: a row of choices (tabs, chips, a program's server tabs) that scrolls sideways with arrow buttons over its faded edges when it is too wide, never with a scroll bar; the arrows show only on a side that hides items and take no room (`Strip_Options`: a step of one item or a page, the fade's width; `ui.strip_view`; the box flag `.Wheel_Sideways`).
