type: Changed

**The confirmation, the prompt, the palette, the sheet of the keys and the setup are views**: they open once, answer once, and need nothing set up: each is a view of the window being built (`ui.views()`). Migration: `ui.confirm(id, &open, o)` each frame becomes `ui.confirm_open(id, o)` once (its options are cloned) and `ui.confirm_answer(id)`; `ui.prompt(id, &open, &field, o)` becomes `ui.prompt_open(id, &field, o)` and `ui.prompt_answer(id)`; `ui.palette_open(&p)` and `ui.palette(&p, commands, disabled, o)` become `ui.palette_set(&p, commands, disabled, o)` when they change, `ui.palette_open(&p)` and `ui.palette_chosen(&p) -> (index, mods, ok)` (`Command_Palette.open` and `.size` go: `ui.palette_is_open(&p)`, the views keep the size); `sheet.open = true` and `ui.shortcuts_sheet(&sheet, id, commands)` become `ui.shortcuts_sheet_open(&sheet, commands)`; `setup.dialog(&d, cfg, em, logo)` becomes `setup.open(&d, cfg, logo)` in a build and `setup.update(&d, cfg)` each frame (`Setup_Options.close` and `Dialog.size` go; a screen of the program's own calls `setup.start`); `prefs_page.install_frame(&in)` loses its `em`. Each answer comes one build later than the old return value.
