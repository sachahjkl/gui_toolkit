**A dialog detaches into a floating window inside its window**: the small Detach icon of a dialog's header, or a drag of the header, makes the view an internal window: not modal, moved by its header, resized by its edges and corners, in front when pressed, with Re-attach and Close in its header; Escape and Ctrl+W close it while it has the focus. The views remember where each one was and whether it floated (`view_present(vs, id, .Internal)` from a program; `View_Options.first = .Internal`). A right click on a view's header opens its menu: Open as a floating window, Re-attach, Close (Detach into a window and the docks come with the next presentations). `View_Labels` has the words of the menu (`reattach`, `window`, `floating`, `dock_left`, `dock_right`, `dock_bottom`). The header of a dialog whose program gives no tools has no tools box.
