type: Added

**`ui.number`**: one widget for a number one sets, of any integer or float type: − and + (held, they repeat), a field where its exact value is typed (applied at Enter or when the keyboard goes, Escape gives it back), Up / Down and Page Up / Page Down with Shift, Ctrl and Alt multipliers, the wheel, optional bounds (a slider with `slider = true`), a step, a format, a unit, `disabled` and a tooltip; a screen reader sees a spin button.
