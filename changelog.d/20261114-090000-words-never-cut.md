type: Changed

**A button's words are never cut**: a width or a height in pixels given to a button is a least (it grows to its words on one line), and a button given less room than its words (a narrow row, a column, a dialog) wraps them onto more lines and grows taller, its padding kept. The layout does this for any row, column or `flow` still too narrow once what may shrink did; `ui.natural_width(box)` is the width a box asks for before the layout, and `ui.button_width(text, glyph)` a button's on one line. `Button_Options.wrap` is gone: every button wraps.
