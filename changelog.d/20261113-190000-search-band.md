**`ui.search_band`: one search band over every list that is searched**: a band from edge to edge in a field's background, a line under it, a magnifier, the field and an optional count; its top corners are those of the box it begins (a dialog, a menu, a popover). The command palette and a menu that searches use it, and a program's palette, picker or sidebar filter gets the same look with one call. `owner_keys` draws a field whose keys come through its owner (a menu).
