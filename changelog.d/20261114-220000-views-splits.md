**Panels split inside a dock zone**: a zone holds several stacks of panels, one above the other at the sides, side by side at the top and the bottom, each with its own tabs (none for a lone panel) and a handle between two. While a view is dragged over a stack, a cross shows at its middle: its centre makes the view a tab there, its sides put it in a new stack before or after. A dock space without content (`ui.views_dock(vs, name, {})`) has a centre zone where panels go. Each zone's axis and its stacks' shares are remembered with the space (`ui.View_Space_Memory`, `views_space_format`, `views_space_parse`), and each view's stack with its place (`View_Place.stack`).
