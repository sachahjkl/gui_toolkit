type: Changed

**One command palette fed by sources** (docs/palette.md): the palette asks its sources, ranks every item together on a chore (the interface's thread matches none, 10 items or 100 000), drops an answer or a ranking for an older query or a former opening, and keeps the selection on its item when rows arrive. One closed table of prefixes for every program: `>` commands, `@` go to, `/` paths, `#` choose, `:` settings, `$` secrets; a character without a source is text. Every row has one shape (icon, title with what the query lit, detail, mark, keys); with nothing typed, Recent and then the commands, the disabled ones greyed and last; a hint row at the foot shows the prefixes and the keys of the selected row; a source that reads says "Sessions: reading…". Enter typed in the same frame as the query chooses from that query's ranking. Tab puts an item's completion in the field. A switch flips and the palette stays open. Ctrl+K and Ctrl+Shift+P are the toolkit's window command, `ui.PALETTE_COMMAND`. Migration:
  - `ui.palette_set(&p, commands, disabled, {memory, names, first, keys, version})` → `ui.palette_commands(&p, commands, disabled, {names, first, keys, version, alternates})`; it adds the commands source and the views' moves source.
  - `ui.palette_chosen(&p) -> (index, mods, ok)` → `ui.palette_chosen(&p) -> (Palette_Choice{source, id, index, command, mods}, ok)`: a command is `c.command >= 0`. Call it at each build: the toolkit's sources act in it.
  - The index decoding by ranges (`i < len(own)`, then the settings, then the moves) goes: the settings and the moves act themselves.
  - `Palette_Options.memory` and `ui.choice_note` for each choice → `ui.palette_memory_open(&p, s, path)`: the palette owns its `Choice_Memory` and notes each choice, the key `<source>:<id>` (`command:<label or name>`, `setting:<name>`).
  - `prefs_page.palette_commands(&prefs, o)` and `prefs_page.show` after the choice → `prefs_page.palette_settings_add(&p, &settings, &prefs, &page, o, open, user)`.
  - `ui.views_commands_update` and `ui.views_command_run` for the palette → nothing: the moves source follows the views of the window the palette is over.
  - `Palette_Options.recent_title` and `.foot` → gone: "Recent" and the hint row are the toolkit's.
  - `ui.key_press(.K, {.Ctrl})` → `ui.command_pressed(ui.PALETTE_COMMAND)` and `ui.palette_toggle(&p)` (or `ui.palette_keys(&p)`).
  - `Command_Palette.rankings` still counts the rankings; `.asks` and `.dropped` count the questions and the answers dropped; `palette_row_count`, `palette_row`, `palette_selected`, `palette_busy` and `ui.rank_matched()` are for tests. `Search_Band_Options.busy` adds "…" after the count.
