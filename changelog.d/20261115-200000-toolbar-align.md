**A tool bar can centre its groups**: `ui.toolbar(id, {align = .Center})` centres each line of a wrapping bar, the last one too, and the groups left in a folding bar in the room before its "…" button and its pinned groups. Cancel and Done stay at the bar's end, in a wrapping bar at the end of its last line. By default (`.Start`) the groups start at the bar's start, as before. In every bar, an item now sits on the middle of its line, a control taller than a tool too.
