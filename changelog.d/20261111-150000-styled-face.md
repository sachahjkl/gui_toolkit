**A widget's bold is the family's own** (a toast's title, a section's name, segmented in words): `ui.styled_face(face, bold, italic)` gives the face the program declared for that family, else the system's family, else a simulated one. The widgets asked `font.styled_face`, which simulated the bold of an embedded face: its strokes thickened by an algorithm, the bar of an e lost its join at small sizes. `rich_set_faces` is now `set_family` (it serves every widget, not rich text alone), and `State.rich_faces` is `families`.
