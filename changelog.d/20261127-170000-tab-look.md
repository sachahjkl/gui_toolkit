**Tabs read as tabs**: in `ui.tabs`, a dock's stacks, a stack of views and the debug window, a line runs along the strip's bottom and the chosen tab rises out of it as one shape with its panel (concave fillets at its feet, rounded top corners in the theme's control radius, the page's colour with the finish's light), no line between them; the other tabs are a faint rounded outline, filled under the pointer, in place of the raised button the chosen tab was. The first tab starts at the strip's edge with a straight side in line with its panel, and the words sit in the middle of the shape. `ui.dock_content_inset()` says where a tab's words start, for a panel's content to line up with them; `ui.tab_line_draw` draws the line across a program's own row that holds `ui.tabs`.
