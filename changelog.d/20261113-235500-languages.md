**Languages**: a program shows its texts in its user's language. `i18n.load` and `i18n.use` read the toolkit's catalogue and the program's; `prefs_page.language_start` follows the Language setting (System: the system's, read by `platform.system_language`), now a choice of English and French. `i18n <language>` writes a catalogue from the code's `tr` calls and `-check` says what is not translated.
