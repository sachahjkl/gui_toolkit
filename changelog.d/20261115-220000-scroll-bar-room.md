**Nothing is drawn under a scroll bar any more**: a view that scrolls lays its content out beside its bar while the bar shows, so the words and buttons at the right end of a row ("Esc close", "Open", a time) stay left of it, in every list, table, menu, the palette, markdown views, the settings page and text areas. When the content fits again the room comes back, and a content that only overflows beside the bar does not take it: the room never flickers. With "Hide the scroll bars" the room stays and the bar fades in it. The new setting "Scroll bars over the content" (`scrolling.scrollbar_overlay`, `ui.set_scrollbar_overlay`, off by default) draws the bars over the content's edge instead. `ui.scrollbar_room` says the room a scroll box keeps; programs no longer leave room for a bar themselves (`ui.scrollbar_gutter()` is now only a bar's thickness).
