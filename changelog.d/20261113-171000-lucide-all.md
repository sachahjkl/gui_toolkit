**`gt:lucide`** names every icon of the font (1876, were about 150 added by hand): `lucide.odin` is generated from `lucide.ttf` by `tk gen_lucide`, each name the font's own. Two constants take the font's name: `TRIANGLE_ALERT` is `ALERT_TRIANGLE`, `SEND_HORIZONTAL` is `SEND_HORIZONAL`.
