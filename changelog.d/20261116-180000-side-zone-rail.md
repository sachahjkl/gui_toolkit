**A side panel folds to a rail.** A stack of views at the left or the right has a Minimise: its zone becomes a narrow column with a Restore and a button per view, and a click on one brings the zone back with that view shown. A side zone also folds by itself when the window is too narrow for it and the content, and unfolds when the window is wide again. In a narrow stack, its Minimise, Maximise and Close are small icons and the view's moves go to its menu, so the buttons are never cut.
