type: Changed

**Every scroll bar takes the desktop's form**: the bars of views, lists, menus, the palette, text areas and bars with marks have a pale trough, an arrow button at each end and a thumb with a gradient across it and a grip, as ShojiWM draws them. The pointer over a bar lights its buttons and gives its thumb the theme's accent; the part under the pointer and the part held go a step deeper. The colours come from the theme: a dark theme gives the desktop's dark bar, a light one its light bar. By default a bar always shows while its content overflows. "Hide the scroll bars" stays an option, off by default, and is now `scrolling.scrollbar_autohide` (it was `scrolling.autohide_scrollbars`): on, a bar shows only while its view moves, while the pointer is over the view or while it is held, and fades otherwise. `ui.set_scrollbar_arrows` and "Scroll bar arrows" are removed: the arrows are always there. An arrow moves the view a line, and repeats after 400 ms then every 60 ms while held; a press on the track moves it a page instead of jumping there. The new setting "Scroll bar width" (`scrolling.scrollbar_size`, `ui.set_scrollbar_size`) is Thin, 9 px and the default, or Wide, 17 px. `ui.scrollbar_paint` takes the bar's parts and its state (`ui.Scrollbar_Parts`, `ui.Scrollbar_Look`); `Text_Edit_Draw.bar_hot` and `bar_held` are `bar_looks`.
