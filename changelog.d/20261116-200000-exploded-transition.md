**The exploded view opens from the page and has a panel.** Turned on, the flat page eases into the turned planes in about 600 ms, and back when it is turned off. A panel at the right sets the mode, the yaw, the pitch, the spread and the zoom, resets the view and closes it. In the Layers mode, a small surface (a pill, a button, a thin band) stays on its parent's plane instead of floating over the words beside it.
