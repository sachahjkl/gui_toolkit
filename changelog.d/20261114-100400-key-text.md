**Keys are named in the language in use everywhere**: `platform.key_text` gives the text of a key, a combination or keys written in English ("Ctrl+K, Ctrl+Shift+P", a portal's "CTRL+SHIFT+a") with the words of the language ("Ctrl+Maj+Impr. écran" in French). Menus, tooltips, the sheet of the keys, the palette, the shortcut field and the settings' lines of a global shortcut use it; Print Screen has a name. `ui.key_tip(label, chord)` gives a tooltip's "Settings (Ctrl+,)" for a control that is no command.
  - `ui.key_name(key)` is removed → `platform.key_text(key)`.
