type: Changed

**A text field's type, procedures and widget have one name**: `ui.Text_Field` is `ui.Line_Edit`, edited by `line_edit` and the `line_edit_*` procedures; `ui.Text_Area` is `ui.Text_Edit`, edited by `text_edit` and the `text_edit_*` procedures. The menu of either is `edit_menu`, a paste of either is `edit_paste`, and the surface around a field is `input_frame`. Both histories are forgotten by `…_clear_history` (`line_edit_clear_history` is new: public). `Field_Snapshot` and `Text_Snapshot` are gone (`Edit_Snapshot`); one `Edit_Kind` (`None`, `Insert`, `Delete`, `Other`) serves both. How a program moves:
  - `Text_Field` → `Line_Edit`; `field_destroy`, `field_string`, `field_set`, `field_clear`, `field_wipe`, `field_can_undo`, `field_can_redo`, `field_undo`, `field_redo`, `field_has_selection`, `field_selection`, `field_select_all`, `field_copy`, `field_cut`, `field_delete`, `field_insert`, `field_paste_answer`, `field_key`, `field_num_step` → `line_edit_` and the same end.
  - `Text_Area` → `Text_Edit`; `area_string`, `area_set`, `area_clear_history`, `area_destroy`, `area_line_count`, `area_undo`, `area_redo` → `text_edit_` and the same end.
  - `field_menu` → `edit_menu`, `field_paste` → `edit_paste`, `field_frame` → `input_frame`.
