**`gt:crash`: crash reports.** One call at the start (`crash.install`) and a program that crashes on Windows or Linux writes a report as it dies: the faulting thread's stack and every other thread's, the modules, the last lines of the log and the settings that differ from their default, in a versioned text that `crash.report_parse` reads. `crash.begin_session` and `crash.end_session` find a session that was killed without a report, and `crash.last_report` tells the next start that the program crashed, so it can offer to open or copy the report. Nothing is sent anywhere.
