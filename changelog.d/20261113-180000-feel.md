**`ui.Feel`**: the interface's delays, repeats and steps in one struct the State holds, read with `ui.feel` and set with `ui.set_feel`, so a settings page can offer each: the tooltip delay, the submenu's hover delay, the menu item's height, the palette's fade, the list's type-ahead, the undo steps and their merge, a held button's repeat, the Shift, Ctrl, Alt and Page steps, the zoom step, and the wheel's lines, a held key's repeat and a fling's end where the system says nothing (`platform.set_input_defaults`). Defaults: `ui.FEEL_DEFAULT`, today's values; docs/feel.md lists each.
