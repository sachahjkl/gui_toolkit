**What the updater does by itself is one mode**: `update.set_auto(u, check, install)` becomes `update.set_auto(u, mode)` with `update.Auto_Mode` `.Off`, `.Check` or `.Install` (an install without a check was no state). `set_auto(u, check = true, install = true)` becomes `set_auto(u, .Install)`; `true, false` becomes `.Check`; `false, false` becomes `.Off`.
