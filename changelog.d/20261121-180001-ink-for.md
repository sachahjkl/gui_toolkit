**`ui.ink_for(row, fg)` and custom-drawn `.Chosen` rows**:
  - `ui.ink_for(row, fg)` exposes the toolkit's selection-ink mapping (title ink, `weak`/`faint`/`soft` ink, or custom
    colour pushed to 4.5:1 contrast, returning `fg` unchanged when `row == nil` or not chosen) for programs that pass an
    explicit `fg` inside a selected row.
  - Custom-drawn `.Chosen` rows (without `.Draw_Background`) now use their `target_bg` selection fill as the ground when
    computing inks, fixing dark ink on Old Head's navy selection.
