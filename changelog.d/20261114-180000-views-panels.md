**Views dock as panels around a window's content**: `ui.views_frame(vs)` around the content gives the window four edges where views dock (`View_Options.home = .Panel`, `home_place`), and `ui.views_dock(vs, name, o)` is a dock space inside the program's own layout. A panel alone at an edge adds nothing to what it draws; two at one edge are tabs. The hover of a panel's top shows its Float and Close icons, a right click there opens its menu, and the menu of every view now docks it left, right or at the bottom. Dragging a dialog's header, a floating window's header, a panel's tab or its title (`ui.view_drag_handle(v, box)`) shows four targets at the edges: let go over one, the view docks there; elsewhere it floats. The handle of each edge resizes it, and the sizes are remembered. The code of a panel or of a floating window reads keys only while the focus is in it, and it reads them before the content; F6 and Shift+F6 go through the content, the panels and the floating windows; Ctrl+W closes a panel. `ui.views_commands(vs)` and `ui.views_command_run` give the palette the same acts ("Dock Files to the right", "Float Files"...), in the language in use. The `docking` and `panes` examples now use views.
