**The graphics device asked at the program's start**: `host.program_start` asks for the device, so that its adapter answers while the program reads its settings and opens its window, and the first frame drawn on the CPU waits less for it. A process that never calls it (a command line's answer, a plugin's child, a headless run) makes no device. `-define:TOOLKIT_EARLY_DEVICE=false` or `TOOLKIT_EARLY_DEVICE=0` turns it off. `render.preload_when_done` gives a device being made a callback for its end (`host.ahead_due` wakes its loop with it).
