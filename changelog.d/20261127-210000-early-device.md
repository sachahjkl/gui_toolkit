**The graphics device asked at the program's start**: a program that links `host` asks for its device before its main, so that its adapter answers while the program starts and opens its window, and the first frame drawn on the CPU waits less for it. `-define:TOOLKIT_EARLY_DEVICE=false` or `TOOLKIT_EARLY_DEVICE=0` turns it off; it is never done in a test. `render.preload_when_done` gives a device being made a callback for its end (`host.ahead_due` wakes its loop with it).
