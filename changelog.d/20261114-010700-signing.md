type: Changed

**Keys as text have one set of names**: the new package `signing` reads and writes the Ed25519 keys of licences and updates. `licence.key_decode`, `update.public_key_decode` and `update.seed_decode` become `signing.key_decode`; `licence.key_encode` and `update.seed_encode` become `signing.key_encode`; `licence.public_key_of` and `update.public_key_of` become `signing.public_key_of`.
