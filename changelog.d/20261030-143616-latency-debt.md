**Frames shown at once no longer queue the later ones** (Direct3D 11): a frame presented without a wait on the swap chain's latency object left it a count beyond its slots, every later wait returned at once and frames queued behind a blocking Present (82 ms input to screen instead of 36 in opencode native).
