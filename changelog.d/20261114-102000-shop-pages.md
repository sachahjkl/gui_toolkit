type: Changed

**`tk_site`: the shop's pages say what Wren's say**: with `Meta.shop`, `build_changelog` writes them, with their canonical address, titles such as "Pricing · name" and the site's footer (Pricing, Privacy, Terms, Refunds, Imprint, the source, the licence). The imprint names the publisher (a trade name and who trades under it), the address, the contact, the web, the SIREN, SIRET and APE code, the register and its date, the VAT, who answers for the content, the hosting, the payments, the code's licence and the consumer disputes. The license terms, the refunds (handled by Paddle, the statutory withdrawal) and the privacy policy take Wren's sections, with a "Legal" label and "Last updated". The pricing page adds the platform, the withdrawal, the checkout and Wren's questions, and its offer lists three lines, each linked to its question; the thank-you page ends with Wren's help block. Every word a reader sees writes "license"; the button says "Buy license".
  - `Shop.seller` is a `Seller` (`name`, `owner`, `status`, `address`, `email`, `url`, `siren`, `siret`, `ape`, `registry`, `registered`, `vat`): `seller = "X", seller_url = u, seller_address = a, seller_email = e, seller_vat = v` → `seller = {name = "X", url = u, address = a, email = e, vat = v, ...}`.
  - New `Shop.platform` and `Shop.network` (HTML): the systems the program runs on and what it connects to, for the privacy policy. New `Shop.repository`: the source, in the footer and the imprint.
  - `Shop.refund_days` is removed: Paddle handles refunds under its policy.
  - `Shop.code_licence` is the licence's whole name (`"MIT"` → `"MIT License"`); `Meta.licence` reads "MIT License" in the footer.
