**`appearance.text.subpixel` is `auto` by default**: text is smoothed per colour stripe as the system smooths its own (ClearType and its order on Windows, fontconfig on Linux, grey on macOS), read once at the start by `prefs_page.standard_register`. `off`, `rgb` and `bgr` stay; a program that followed the system itself (`font.system_subpixel_text` while the setting was at its default) drops that code.
