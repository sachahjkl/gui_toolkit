**`tk release` and `release.ship`** take `patch`, `minor`, `major` or a version, and the number is the next after the last tag on origin. They publish from any clean checkout (a branch, a worktree, a detached HEAD), push master and the tag at once (atomic), and start again with the next number when origin moved meanwhile: a tag never goes out without its commit. **`release.publish`**, **`release.version_next`**: the step they share.
