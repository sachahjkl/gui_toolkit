**`examples/showcase`**: a scene per feature, each made to be looked at: the intents and hues, the numbers and ranges, the command palette, a menu that searches, the dialogs (`confirm`, `prompt`), the settings page with every standard set, a node graph and its inspector, the toasts, every theme and finish. Its data is fixed: each scene is the same picture at each run. `--scene=`, `--theme=`, `--light`, `--finish=`, `--page=`, `--advanced`, `--headless` and `--size=` choose what it shows.
