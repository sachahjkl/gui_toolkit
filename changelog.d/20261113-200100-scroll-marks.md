**`ui.mark_rail` is now `ui.scroll_marks`: marks on the view's own scroll bar.** The marks are short bars across the track of the vertical bar, as an editor's overview ruler, instead of dots on a second column beside it. The marks are thin, muted ticks; those within the thumb's span are drawn over it, a little brighter, so it says where the view is among them. Marks closer than 6 px are drawn as one; the pointer over it shows the first line of each of its items. The mark under the pointer grows, in the full accent. The bar is wider when it carries marks and widens more under the pointer. A press on the thumb over a mark drags the thumb. The filter is a magnifier button above the bar (docs/scroll-marks.md).
  - Renamed: `Mark_Rail` → `Scroll_Marks`, `Rail_Mark` → `Scroll_Mark`, `Mark_Rail_Options` → `Scroll_Marks_Options` (without `no_filter` and `filter_w`), `mark_rail_*` → `scroll_marks_*`, `mark_rail_dot` → `scroll_marks_at`. `scroll_marks` takes no box of its own size: it draws on the view's bar.
