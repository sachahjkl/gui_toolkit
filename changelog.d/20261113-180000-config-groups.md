**Preference groups from the name**: the segments of a preference's name but the last are its groups (`appearance.text.gamma` is in `appearance`, then `appearance.text`). `config.path_of(name)` gives them, `config.title_register(prefs, path, title, order)` gives a group its heading and its place, `config.group_title` reads it (else it is made from the last segment), and `config.groups_of(prefs, parent)` lists the groups in a group in their order ([docs/config.md](docs/config.md)).
