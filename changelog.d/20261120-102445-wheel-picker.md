**`ui.duration_field`, `ui.parts_field`, `ui.wheel`, `ui.wheel_number`, `ui.wheels`**: a duration, a time of day or any value of a few parts in one field, each part typed or stepped on its own (Left, Right, Up, Down, digits that go on by themselves), and a click opens the parts' drums below it. A drum turns with the wheel, a drag and a throw, a click on a row and the keys; it rests on a row with a detent and gives one change, at rest. The showcase's Wheels scene shows them.
