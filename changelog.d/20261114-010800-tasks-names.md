**Work pools: one word per thing**: `tasks.stop(p, drop)` names its handler `lost`, as `ui.task` does. `tasks.ask` and `tasks.busy` become `tasks.job_ask` and `tasks.job_busy`; `tasks.state` and `tasks.State` become `tasks.counts` and `tasks.Counts`. `tasks.Options` becomes `tasks.Pool_Options` and `tasks.Add` becomes `tasks.Add_Options` (its parameter `o`).
