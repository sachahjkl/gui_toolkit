**The bevel finish and the Old Head theme**: `Finish.Bevel` draws Windows 95's surfaces: two-pixel, two-tone bevels around raised keys and sunken fields, a pressed key sunken, square corners, a dotted focus rectangle, no shadow, halo nor hover light, scroll bars with a pale track and raised arrows and thumb, text selected white on an opaque accent. `Old Head` (one grey, white fields, navy, the 16-colour hues) is made for it and brings it whatever the program's finish.
