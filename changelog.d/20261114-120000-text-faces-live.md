type: Changed

**The family settings apply live, in every program**: `prefs_page.standard_watch` now applies `appearance.text.ui_font`, `mono_font` and `prose_font` itself, with no restart. The family's face is loaded on a chore (with its bold and italic) and swapped in when it comes, in every window, views' windows included; Default puts the program's own face back at once.
  - `ui.Text_Face`, `ui.set_text_faces`, `ui.use_text_family`, `ui.state_text_face`, `ui.text_face`, `ui.program_text_face`, `ui.text_family`, `ui.text_faces_serial`, `ui.text_font_source`, `ui.prose_font`, and `ui.Family_Face` with `family_face_set` and `family_face_destroy` for a face of a program's own.
  - `Standard_Targets.faces` gives the program's own faces, `Standard_Targets.font_source` the families' source; `Page_Options.default_fonts`, `Default_Fonts` and `Page_Options.font_source` are removed.
  - `ui.mono_font()`, rich text, text edits and markdown tables read the chosen monospace face; a markdown view's text is in the reading face when `View_Options.prose` is 0.
