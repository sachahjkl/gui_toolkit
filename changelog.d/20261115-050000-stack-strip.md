**A stack's tab strip with "+", moves and window-like buttons**: `View_Options.tabs_always` shows a stack's strip for one view (a terminal). `View_Options.new_tab: proc(data: rawptr)` puts a "+" right after the last tab: the program opens a new instance, which joins the stack as its last tab; the same act is in the band's menu and the palette, in `new_tab_label`'s words. The shown view's moves (Float, Open in a window, Re-attach) sit right after the tabs, its tools after them, and at the right end Minimise (top and bottom zones, remembered), Maximise (the stack fills the dock space; again or Escape restores; `ui.views_space_maximised`) and Close (every view of the stack), drawn as the window's caption buttons.
