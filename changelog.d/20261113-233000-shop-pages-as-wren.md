**`tk_site`: the shop's pages say what Wren's say**: the imprint names the publisher (a trade name and who trades under it), the address, the contact, the web, the SIREN, SIRET and APE code, the register and its date, the VAT, who answers for the content, the hosting, the payments, the code's licence and the consumer disputes; the license terms, the refunds (handled by Paddle, the statutory withdrawal) and the privacy policy (the program, the site, the purchase, the rights) take Wren's sections, with a "Legal" label and "Last updated". The pricing page adds the platform, the withdrawal, the checkout and Wren's questions, and its offer lists Wren's three lines, each linked to its question; the thank-you page thanks the buyer for supporting the program and ends with Wren's help block. Every word a reader sees writes "license", the button and the nav's last entry say "Buy license" (a small accent button), and the footers list Pricing, Privacy, Terms, Refunds, Imprint, as Wren's.
  - `Shop.seller` is a `Seller` (`name`, `owner`, `status`, `address`, `email`, `url`, `siren`, `siret`, `ape`, `registry`, `registered`, `vat`): `seller = "X", seller_url = u, seller_address = a, seller_email = e, seller_vat = v` → `seller = {name = "X", url = u, address = a, email = e, vat = v, ...}`.
  - New `Shop.platform` and `Shop.network` (HTML): the systems the program runs on, and what it connects to, for the privacy policy.
  - `Shop.refund_days` is removed: Paddle handles refunds under its policy, as on Wren's site.
  - `Shop.code_licence` is the licence's whole name (`"MIT License"`); `Meta.licence` reads "MIT License" in the footer.
