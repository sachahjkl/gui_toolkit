**A selection dragged to the edge of a scroll view scrolls it** (`ui.drag_autoscroll`, `ui.drag_autoscroll_own`): near or past the edge, the view scrolls toward the pointer, slowly in a band of 1.5 em inside the edge and faster past it, and the selection extends to what scrolls in, so a selection can span several screens. Text selection, `text_edit`, `line_edit` and the terminal's grid do it; nested views scroll the innermost one that can still move that way.
