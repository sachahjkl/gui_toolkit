**A scroll mark's card**: the pointer over a mark shows its name (`Scroll_Mark.label`, "#12"), when its item was made (`Scroll_Mark.time_ms`, read on `Scroll_Marks_Options.now_ms`, `offset_minutes`, `hours12`) and the first three lines of its text; a merged mark's card has a line for each item with its time. `timefmt.moment` says when something was: "today 14:32", "yesterday 09:10", "Nov 10, 14:32".
