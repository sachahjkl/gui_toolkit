**Rows of tabs** (a stack of views, a dock's stack, `ui.tabs`): too long for their room, the tabs first narrow, their words cut with an ellipsis down to `ui.TAB_TITLE_LEAST_EM`, before the strip scrolls; a tab cut by an arrow to a sliver of its outline is hidden whole; a strip resized brings its chosen tab back into view; a narrow stack keeps its Close inside the window. `ui.tab_title_width` sizes a tab's words for a program's own row of tabs.
