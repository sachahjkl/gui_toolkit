type: Fixed

**Letters typed right after a view opens are no longer lost, and nothing waits**: a view that opened as a floating window or as a panel lost the letters typed in its first frames (only a dialog kept them). Every view is now built in the frame it opens, so the field it focuses has the very next keys; a view opening in a window of its own gets what was typed before its window showed. A panel whose dock space is not shown now appears at once as a floating window, and goes to its space when the space shows.
