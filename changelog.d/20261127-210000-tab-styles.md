**Tabs take the surface they frame, in a style per context**: a dock's stacks and a stack of views are an Underline (the strip on the panel's own ground, no line nor border before it, an accent bar under the shown tab); `ui.tabs` and `ui.tab_panel` are Folders (the chosen tab is the page's surface, one outline round both, the other tabs words with no outline); a small panel's switcher asks for `.Segmented`, a header band for `.Header`. A content names its ground once (`Dock_Options.surface`, `View_Options.surface`, `Tab_Panel_Options.surface`, `ui.tabs_over`) and the toolkit paints its body, strip and tab with it: no double border, no cut, no pasted tab. `Dock_Options.tab_style`, `View_Dock_Options.tab_style`, `Tabs_Options.style` choose another. `ui.tabs_ex` gives a tab its own content (`Tabs_Options.content`), a "+" after the tabs (`after`), the tab whose menu was asked by a right click, the Menu key or Shift+F10 (`Tabs_Result.menu`, `menu_at`) and a middle click (`closed`). The debug window's tabs are an Underline; the edged finish no longer lights a dock's strip.
