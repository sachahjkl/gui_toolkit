**Tab styles and the surface they frame**: `ui.set_tab_style` (folder, underline, segmented, header, beside the silhouette), `ui.tab_surface` and `Dock_Options.surface` (a content names its ground and edge once; the chosen tab, the strip and the dock's body take them), `ui.tab_panel` (a row of tabs and its page as one framed surface), `ui.tabs_ex` (a tab's own content, a slot after the tabs, the tab whose menu was asked by a right click or the keyboard, a middle click).
