**`ui.mark_rail` is `ui.scroll_marks`: marks on the view's own scroll bar** (docs/scroll-marks.md). The marks are thin, muted ticks across the track of the vertical bar, as an editor's overview ruler, instead of dots on a column beside it; those within the thumb's span are drawn over it, a little brighter. Marks closer than 6 px are drawn as one. The mark under the pointer grows, in the full accent, and shows its card: its name (`Scroll_Mark.label`, "#12"), when its item was made (`Scroll_Mark.time_ms`, said with `Scroll_Marks_Options.now_ms`, `offset_minutes` and `hours12`) and the first three lines of its text; a merged mark's card has a line per item with its time. The bar is wider when it carries marks and widens more under the pointer; a press on the thumb over a mark drags the thumb. The filter is a magnifier button above the bar. `timefmt.moment` says when something was: "today 14:32", "yesterday 09:10", "Nov 10, 14:32".
  - `Mark_Rail` → `Scroll_Marks`, `Rail_Mark` → `Scroll_Mark`, `Mark_Rail_Options` → `Scroll_Marks_Options` (without `no_filter` and `filter_w`), `mark_rail_*` → `scroll_marks_*`, `mark_rail_dot` → `scroll_marks_at`.
  - `scroll_marks` takes no box of its own size: it draws on the view's bar.
