**`ui.button` and `ui.icon_button`** take an `intent` (their colour), a `face` (`Button_Face`: raised, ghost, toggled), `disabled`, `lit` (drawn as under the pointer while its menu is open) and a `tip` (a tooltip, read as their description); `ui.icon_button` also an `icon_font` and a `name` for a screen reader (else its tip). A toggled button is held down and read as pressed.
