type: Changed

**Widgets never cut their words**: a button given less room than its words (a narrow row, a column, a dialog) wraps them onto more lines between words and grows taller, its padding kept; a word wider than the room makes the button wider. The layout does this for any row, column or `flow` still too narrow once what may shrink did.
  - A width or a height in pixels given to a button is a least: it grows to its words on one line. `ui.button_width(text, glyph)` is a button's width on one line; `ui.natural_width(box)` the width a box asks for before the layout.
  - `Button_Options.wrap` is removed: every button wraps.
  - A popover's `width` is a least: it grows to what is in it.
  - The settings page's sidebar is as wide as its longest page title in the language in use, up to its most (past it, a title wraps); its handle does not make it narrower. The settings' search shows its placeholder whole.
