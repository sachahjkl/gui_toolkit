**A port of a flow graph is one value**: `flowgraph.fit_port(g, cat, node, port, output, kind)` becomes `fit_port(g, cat, {node, port, side}, kind)` and `node_add_linked(g, cat, kind, pos, node, port, output)` becomes `node_add_linked(g, cat, kind, pos, {node, port, side})`, with `flowgraph.Port_Ref` and `flowgraph.Side` (`.In`, `.Out`). From a widget's `ui.Graph_Port_Ref`: `{from.node, from.port, flowgraph.Side(from.side)}`.
