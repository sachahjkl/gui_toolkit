type: Changed

**A side zone's fold button is the view's to place, and its rail opens by a pull.** `ui.view_fold_button(v)` draws the zone's Minimise when it is expanded and its Restore when it is collapsed, in a row the view already has. Put it at the same place in both forms and it stays under the pointer. The row that 0.300 added above a view with `rail_em` is gone: such a view must call the button in its compact form, and the toolkit's rail no longer draws a Restore for it. A strip leaves its Minimise out when the view shows the button. A minimised side zone (both rails) keeps its edge line against the content. Pull that edge outward and the zone opens and follows the pointer; drag an open zone down to its rail and it folds.
