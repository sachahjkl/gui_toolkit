type: Changed

**One `ui.button`**: `button(id, text, icon, o: Button_Options) -> Signal` replaces `button_icon`, `icon_button` and `hint_action`; `nav_item` is gone (the settings page's sidebar lines are buttons). `id` is the id alone ("add" or "###add"); the words are `text`, the glyph `icon`; the options (`intent`, `face`, `width`, `height`, `quiet`, `wrap`, `left`, `lit`, `disabled`, `tip`, `icon_font`) are fields of `Button_Options`. Words, with a glyph or not, are always the toolkit's row: its size from `o.width`, `o.height` or `next` (a `text_size(pad)` width: the words and `pad` on each side), its words aligned as the style or `next` says, in their font and size. How a program moves:
  - `button("Add###add", intent = .Primary)` → `button("add", "Add", o = {intent = .Primary})`. Without a size from `next`, the button is now as wide as its words and padding and a line of the toolkit's height, its corners rounded.
  - `button_icon(id, glyph, text, ...)` → `button(id, text, glyph, {...})`; with no glyph, `button(id, text, o = {...})`. `hue`, `solid` and `icon_color` are gone.
  - `icon_button(id, glyph, tip = t, ...)` → `button(id, icon = glyph, o = {tip = t, ...})` (a ghost unless `face` says otherwise). `name` is gone: `ui.a11y({name = ...})` before it names it.
  - `hint_action(id, text, icon, icon_font, intent)` → `button(id, text, icon, {face = .Link, icon_font = icon_font, intent = intent})`.
  - `icon(glyph, width, fg)` takes the font as a fourth parameter, `icon_font`; `card(title, icon)` names its glyph `icon`.
