**A segmented control is as wide as its labels**: with no width given, `ui.segmented` makes every segment as wide as its widest label with its padding, so no label is cut in any language; a width given is kept. `ui.segmented_width(labels)` gives that width to a layout that places the control before it is built; the settings' choices use it. A segment under the pointer, pressed or focused shows a soft fill with the place and the rounded corners of the key, inside the track (it was a box as tall as the track with corners of its own).
