**The AppUserModelID is derived, never written**: `platform.app_user_model_id(publisher, name)` makes it (PascalCase words of ASCII letters and digits, joined by a dot: "Froment Software" and "opencode native" give `FromentSoftware.OpencodeNative`). gt:toolbox gives the build its `GT_AUMID` from the Project's publisher and title (`project_aumid`; `Project.aumid` is removed), and gt:desktop's `Identity.aumid` is derived from its vendor and its name when empty (`identity_aumid`), so the windows and the shortcuts group in the taskbar with no string written twice. A Project without a publisher has none.
