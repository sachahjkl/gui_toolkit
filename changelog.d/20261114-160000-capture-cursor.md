**`capture.set_cursor`, `capture.cursor_support`**: a still picture (`output`, `window`, `desktop`) can show the pointer. Wayland's compositors draw it (`ext_image_copy_capture_v1`, `zwlr_screencopy_v1`); on Windows it is drawn over an output's picture (`GetCursorInfo`, `DrawIconEx`) and asked of Windows.Graphics.Capture for a window. The portal and X11 cannot: `cursor_support` says so (`backend_draws_cursor`). `capture.patch_clip` is the pure cut it uses. See docs/capture.md, "The pointer in a picture".
