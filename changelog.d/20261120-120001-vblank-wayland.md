**Vblank timing on Wayland** (`platform.vblank_timing`, `platform.vblank_ask`, `Vblank_Timing`): the compositor's presentation answers (`wp_presentation`) say when each picture reached the screen. `render.measured_refresh_hz` and `render.vblank_grid` now answer on Wayland, a frame after a rest takes the measured refresh as its dt, and `render.present_totals` counts the pictures the compositor showed, not the presents. X11 has none yet.
