**`hue_picker` and `alpha_picker` say their axis**: `hue_picker(id, &hue, axis)` and `alpha_picker(id, hue, sat, val, &alpha, axis)` take a required `axis: Axis` in place of `horizontal` (whose defaults were opposite): `hue_picker(id, &h)` → `hue_picker(id, &h, .Y)`; `alpha_picker(..., &a)` → `alpha_picker(..., &a, .X)`; `horizontal = true` → `.X`.
