**Keys are named in the language in use everywhere.** `platform.key_text` gives the text of a key, a combination or keys written in English ("Ctrl+K, Ctrl+Shift+P", a portal's "CTRL+SHIFT+a") with the words of the language: "Ctrl+Maj+Impr. écran" in French. Menus, tooltips, the sheet of the keys, the palette, the shortcut field and the settings' lines of a global shortcut all use it. Print Screen has a name ("Impr. écran" in French). `ui.key_tip(label, chord)` gives a tooltip's "Settings (Ctrl+,)" for a control that is no command. `ui.key_name` is removed: use `platform.key_text(key)`.
