**A window resized on a 60 Hz screen keeps up with the hand** (wgpu, Linux): a resize's frames were queued in Fifo behind one another, each shown a refresh or two after it was made, so the content trailed the compositor's edge by 33 ms on a 60 Hz screen where 144 Hz hid it. While the window is resized its frames now go out in a mailbox, the newest size shown at the next refresh; once the resize is over they wait for the screen again.
