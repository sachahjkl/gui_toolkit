**The menu of a text area**: a right press in a `ui.text_edit` opens the same menu as a `ui.line_edit`'s (Undo, Redo, Cut, Copy, Paste, Delete, Select all, their keys and icons, a search); `menu = false` leaves it to the program. `ui.field_menu` takes a `ui.Edit_Target`: a `Text_Field` or a `Text_Area`.
