**No second line under a frame's top edge**: in the machined, edged, gradient and glass finishes, a header, a row or a stripe inside a frame, a card or a well no longer gets the lit rim of a raised face. Such a box is part of the face it sits in where it meets that face's top edge or runs across its whole width, so the face's own edge is the only line there. A table's header no longer shows a second line just under its frame's border. Buttons, cards, chips and dialogs that stand on their own keep their rim.
