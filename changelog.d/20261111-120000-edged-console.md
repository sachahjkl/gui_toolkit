**The edged finish and the Console theme**: `Finish.Edged` draws the machined reliefs crisp, nothing blurred: a dark hairline around a face, a lit line along its top, a shade along its bottom; a well with dark lines under its top and a lit lip; square corners, except what is round by its shape (a radio, a switch, a slider's knob: `shape_rad`). `Console` (blue black, the hues of a console) is made for it. With them, pieces of a dense dashboard: `section_header` (a stripe, a title in capitals in its hue, a note, a value), `tile` (a tile of colour with its words), `stacked_bar` (a bar cut in parts with their words), `title_strip` (a title on the accent fading out, a red block that closes), `hue(.Cyan)` (the hues of the theme shown) and `relief_face`.
