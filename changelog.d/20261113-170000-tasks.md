**`gt:tasks`, `ui.task`, `ui.chores`**: work off the interface's thread and its result back on it. `tasks.Pool` (its order or the newest first, urgent work, timed work, more threads while some hang, a pool of no thread a test drives by its own clock) and `tasks.Job` (asks that merge); `ui.task(s, work, done, data, lost)` runs `work` on the program's chores and `done` at the next build, and never touches a State destroyed meanwhile ([docs/tasks.md](docs/tasks.md)).
